Software that respects you.

Developer tools and apps, made with care and kept that way. Starting with Dart and Flutter.

Always

  • Does one job well, and says plainly what it does.
  • Works without an account wherever one isn't needed.
  • Fails loudly, with a message that says what to do next.
  • Is tested well past what it strictly needs.

Never

  • Tracks you, or what you do with it.
  • Shows you ads, or sells anything it learns.
  • Sends your data anywhere you didn't ask it to.
  • Nags you to upgrade.

Tools

Testing

grit

Property-based testing for Dart.

Say what should hold for every input. grit finds an input that breaks it, then shrinks it to the smallest case that still fails, so the bug is plain to see. Its shrinker is checked against a model proved in Lean.

Testing

muster

Real codebases to test your Dart tools against.

Pin the projects your tool should handle, and muster brings each one to that commit, resolved and ready to read. Your first test run needs no setup, and no fetch ever fails quietly.
